# @mindpeeker/coincidence

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- New package, first release on npm. Exact probabilities of coincidences under explicit null models, with no dependencies: the birthday problem and its generalisations (unequal category probabilities, k-fold matches via Levin's method, near matches, several attributes), the Diaconis–Mosteller approximations next to the exact values, the law of truly large numbers, Fisher's 1924 graded-match scores, and an exact clustering test for event series. The numbers say how cheap a coincidence is under a stated model; they are not evidence for or against any meaning attached to it.
