# Code of conduct

This project has adopted the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make
participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, and to act and
interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy
community.

The Covenant applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and
any other channel where someone represents the project) and in public spaces when an
individual is officially representing the project.

The subject matter of this repository includes contested research. Disagreeing with a claim,
a method or a conclusion is welcome and expected; criticise ideas and evidence, not people.

## Reporting

Report conduct that violates the Covenant to the project maintainer privately:

- open a private report at
  <https://github.com/Polobase/mindpeeker-sdk/security/advisories/new> and start its title
  with "Code of conduct" (these reports are visible only to the maintainer), or
- if the matter is not sensitive, open an issue at
  <https://github.com/Polobase/mindpeeker-sdk/issues>.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and
security of the reporter will be respected. The maintainer follows the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
(correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
